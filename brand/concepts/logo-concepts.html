A · Loop wordmark · Recommended

The double L is the loop

The two l's join top and bottom into one closed loop, set in copper. The odd spelling becomes the logo, so nobody reads it as a typo. The loop alone becomes the favicon (right). Every other letter stays plain Cabinet Grotesk.

B · Two-L monogram

Two L's chasing each other

One L and one L turned over make a square that never quite closes. Blocky and engineered, it reads at 16px and suits app icons, stickers and machined parts. Pairs with A as its icon.

C · Open loop

Endless, with a way in

A single-weight infinity with one gap, so the line is always about to continue. Strongest in motion: the gap travels around the loop as a loader or a scroll cue. Quieter than A and B as a static mark.

D · Current ribbon mark

Keep and refine

The calligraphic infinity already on the site. It is elegant but leans calm and could belong to many studios. If kept, thicken the hairline tips so they survive at 24px and below.
