## Match on the session, not the profile

Glid is an Endlls product design exploration for sports matchmaking. Instead of opening on profiles and chats, the product leads with place, time, and skill—so people connect around an actual session before they message.

## An iOS surface with room to breathe

The iOS client is built in SwiftUI with a glassmorphic visual language across Home, Matches, and Explore. Key screens include a hero join flow, event detail, and map / live activity views that keep the session in context while someone is on the move.

> Place. Time. Skill. Then talk.

## Stack under the glass

Native Swift and SwiftUI sit on a Rust `shared_core`, with Apple and Google authentication and MapKit for location-aware sessions. The project is currently paused; there are no launch metrics to report.

## Portfolio framing

This case study presents Glid as Endlls studio product work—identity, creative direction, and digital experience—rather than as external client delivery.
