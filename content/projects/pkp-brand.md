## A system for community moments

PKP Tender Hearts Foundation runs frequent public gatherings—vigils, blood drives, rights workshops—that need to feel related without locking into a finished brand book. Endlls treated the work as campaign and art direction: a living visual system for events, grounded in the foundation’s existing logo colors.

## Tokens from the mark

Web and print tokens start from the logo magenta and maroon. The same palette carries across flyer layouts and the related website experience, so a candlelight vigil poster and a donation page can share one recognizable temperature.

> One mark. Many evenings of gathering.

## Flyers in the field

Campaign pieces supported three community events in Northern Virginia:

- **Candle LIGHT vigil** — August 30 at the Fairfax Government Center, for Nepal flood victims, in partnership with GMU NSA and L2D.
- **Blood Drive** — August 8 at Inova Centremed, Centreville (code 8885).
- **Know Your Rights** — September 13 in Centreville, with Public Defender Amy Jordan.

Each piece carries the same magenta–maroon system while staying clear enough for street and venue distribution.

## Working guidelines, not a locked book

Brand guidance lives as a practical Notion reference for ongoing events and web—thin by design. This case study frames art direction and campaign application rather than a completed brand-book PDF.
