## Space to breathe

Aster House is a fictional coastal retreat imagined for people seeking a slower pace. The identity begins with restraint: a soft material palette, generous margins, and a voice that leaves room for the landscape.

## An identity shaped by place

We developed a flexible system spanning guest materials, editorial stories, and a calm digital presence. Every element is designed to feel considered without becoming precious.

> The result is a brand that feels discovered rather than imposed.
